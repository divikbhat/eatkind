About Me

Palak Jain

MSc Clinical Nutrition | Certified Diabetes Educator

Palak Jain is a dedicated Clinical Nutritionist and Certified Diabetes Educator with over 5 years of experience across prestigious hospitals and innovative health-tech startups. With a Bachelors of Honors in Nutrition and Dietetics from Punjab Agricultural University and a Master’s in Clinical Nutrition from SNDT University, Mumbai, Palak’s expertise is rooted in deep academic and clinical rigor.

Her career began in the intensive clinical settings of Max Hospital (Saket, Delhi) and CMCH Ludhiana, where she managed complex chronic diseases through medical nutrition therapy. Transitioning into the digital health space with startups like Sugarfit, she has been instrumental in the field of diabetes reversal, helping clients achieve metabolic health through data-backed lifestyle interventions.

My Vision

Palak believes that the key to a healthier life lies in sustainability. In an industry often filled with "quick fixes," her mission is to deliver science-based nutrition guidance tailored to the unique biological and lifestyle needs of every individual.

"I believe in achieving your health goals in a way that builds habits for a lifetime. My goal is to empower you with the tools to make lasting changes, ensuring your journey to wellness is as enduring as it is effective."
